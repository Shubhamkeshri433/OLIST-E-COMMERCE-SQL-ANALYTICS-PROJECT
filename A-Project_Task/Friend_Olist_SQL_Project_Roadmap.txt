OLIST E-COMMERCE SQL ANALYTICS PROJECT
ROADMAP — FRIEND
Dataset: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

PROJECT DIRECTION
Use the real Olist dataset and analyze only business areas supported by it.
Focus on data profiling, quality, products, categories, sellers, reviews,
delivery/logistics, geography, business insights and documentation.

Do NOT invent returns, promotions, profit, or unsupported fields.

==================================================
